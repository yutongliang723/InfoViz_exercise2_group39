How to interact

  • Click a country on the map or PCA to show its time series.
  • Ctrl + click to select multiple countries or PCA dots.
  • Deselect by clicking on the country or PCA dots.
  • Deselect all by clicking "Clear Highlights" button.
  • Brush in the PCA plot to select a group.
  • Hover on the map, PCA dots, or lines to see details in a tooltip.
  • Scroll to zoom the map; drag to pan; use home icon to reset the view.
  • Indicator dropdown updates all three views simultaneously.
  • Year slider changes the map and PCA in real time.
